Copy the contents of the Nasal folder to your aircraft Nasal folder.

Add this to your nasal section of your set file:-

<gtx327>

<file>Aircraft/YOUR AIRCRAFT/Nasal/gtx-327.nas</file>
 ! edit to your aircrafts own nasal  location.
</gtx327>

Add the GTX to your Instruments or panel.xml:-

<model>
<name>gtx-327</name>

<path>Aircraft/YOUR AIRCRAFT/Models/Instruments/gtx-327/gtx-327.xml</path>
  ! Alter path to suit your aircraft.
<offsets>
			
<x-m>o</x-m>
		! Alter XYZ to fit your cockpit.	
<y-m>o</y-m>
			
<z-m>o</z-m>
		
</offsets>
	
</model>
